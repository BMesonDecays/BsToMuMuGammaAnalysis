5. CRAB submission: ParkingDoubleMuonLowMass 2024 B/C/D MINIAOD
======================================================================
Everything is ready to submit, and a crash that would have hit almost
every 2024 job is fixed. Nothing has been submitted yet. The script, the
2024 JSON URL and 2000-event local runs on 2024C and 2024D files all work,
with no exceptions.

What was set up
- crab_data/multicrab_DATA_2024BCD.py submits 24 CRAB tasks, one per
  dataset: ParkingDoubleMuonLowMass0-7 for each of three eras.
  - 2024B uses PromptReco, the only processing there is.
  - 2024C and 2024D use 2024CDEReprocessing. MINIv6NANOv15 was skipped
    because it was made with CMSSW_15_0_2, which CMSSW_14_1_1 most likely
    can't read.
  - Jobs are filtered with the full-year 2024 Golden JSON
    (Cert_Collisions2024_378981_386951_Golden.json). Golden rather than
    the Muon JSON, because the ECAL is needed for the photon.
  - CRAB sizes jobs automatically, at about 3 hours each.
  - Output goes to /store/user/psajdak/crab_out
    (= /eos/user/p/psajdak/crab_out on CERNBox).
  - Size: about 1.29 billion events (about 67 TB of input), roughly 1300
    CPU-hours, about 250 GB of output.
- test/runRadiativeAnalysis_MINIAOD_test.py: progress is reported every
  1000 events instead of every 10, and KalmanVertexUpdator warnings are
  capped at 10 per job.

Crash that was fixed
- On a 2024D file the analyzer stopped with "InvalidCluster: The cluster
  has no crystals!". About 5% of slimmedPhotons in 2024 MINIAOD (both B
  and D) have a supercluster with no crystal list, and
  EcalClusterLazyTools::covariancesXYZ throws on those.
- src/TripleObjectVertexFit.cc: these photons now skip the Bs fit. They
  are still stored, with bsFitSuccessful = false, so about 5% of photons
  in data lose the Bs fit. No effect on the private AOD MC.

2. Photon MVA cut
======================================================================
RadiativeAnalysis does not cut on the photon MVA anywhere. The score is
only stored in the photonMVA branch. photonMVACut = -0.07 exists only as
a leftover parameter in the older ntupler configs. Photon selection:
supercluster present, supercluster energy >= 1 GeV, |eta| < 2.5, and
dR(gamma, mumu) < 0.5. The 10 GeV edge in photonPt is the minimum photon
ET of the standard AOD "photons" reconstruction.
Recommendation: choose the MVA threshold offline. (Open: add a
configurable cut to the analyzer if wanted.)

1. Muon pT cut moved from the resonance flag to MuonSelector
======================================================================
Why: about 20% of MC candidates were not flagged as J/psi. 86% of those
had one muon with pT < 3 GeV (the J/psi flag required both muons above
3 GeV); the rest were outside the +-150 MeV mass window. Unflagged
candidates miss the J/psi mass constraint, and their bsMass is about 50%
wider (RMS 0.232 vs 0.158).

Changes
- src/MuonSelector.cc: passMuonSelection requires pT > muonPtCut for both
  muons, before the dimuon vertex fit.
- interface/MuonSelector.h: muonPtCut = 3.0 GeV (public constant).
- src/ReferenceResonance.cc: resonance flag is decided by mass only.
- interface/ReferenceResonance.h: removed the unused Constants::PtCut.

Check (job-000 files, 8520 MC events): Bs candidates 12 -> 10, J/psi 9 ->
9, unflagged 3 -> 1, with a soft muon 2 -> 0. Muon pairs stored in the
tree 702 -> 277. Expected loss on the full MC sample: about 18% of Bs
candidates.
